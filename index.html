Troubleshooting

If you experience issues, a quick restart usually works:

  1. Head to the 1st floor TV cabinet, open the 2nd door from the right.
  2. Flip the RED light switch on the power strip to OFF.
  3. Wait 30 seconds, then flip it back to ON. Give it a few minutes to reboot.
Provider: Spectrum

Entertainment

How to Use the TVs

Large screens are located on the Main Floor and 1st Level. Use the black remote for Smart TV apps. Please sign out of personal accounts before check-out.

Cable TV: Power on the cable box (center TV cabinet) with the silver remote. Use the black remote to select HDMI 1 (Main Floor) or HDMI 2 (1st Level). Control channels with the silver remote.

Bedroom TVs: Open the Spectrum app installed on the TV for instant cable access.

Bluetooth Speaker

Find the sliding switch on the side to turn on Bluetooth. Pair your phone with BA-02. Please keep the speaker indoors at all times.

Home Care

Air Conditioning

Controls are on each floor. Please keep windows/doors closed when AC is on. To prevent system freeze-ups, set to AUTO/COOL at 70-75 degrees. When departing, please set to 65.

Refrigerators

May take up to 48 hours to re-cool after filling with groceries. Keep doors closed as much as possible!

Vacuum Cleaners

Found in laundry closets or bedroom closets.

Pools & Hot Tubs

Pools/tubs are ready after 4:00 p.m. on check-in day. Vendors perform mid-week chemical checks.

Strict Rule: No spray sunscreen near the pool. It clogs filters and damages the surface. Apply away from the water and let dry first.

Pool Heat: Operates when ambient air is above 55°F. If overnight temps drop below 50°F, heat cannot be guaranteed.

Before You Leave

Trash: Place cans at the street the evening before pick-up (facing the street).

  • May 1 - Oct 31: Sundays & Wednesdays (for Mon/Thu pickup)
  • Nov 1 - Apr 30: Guest must roll to street

Damages: Report accidental damage immediately to the office to expedite repairs.

Checkout: Use the Remote Check Out Button on the Happy Stays app.
