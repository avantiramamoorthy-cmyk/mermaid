Troubleshooting

If you have issues, go to the 1st floor TV cabinet (2nd door from right). Flip the RED switch on the power strip OFF, wait 30 seconds, then flip it back ON. Our provider is Spectrum.

Television & Entertainment

Main Floor & 1st Level: Use the Black Remote for Smart TV apps (Netflix/Disney+). Please remember to sign out before leaving.

Cable TV: Power on the box using the Silver Remote (located in the center cabinet). Use the Black remote to select HDMI 1 (Main Floor) or HDMI 2 (1st Level), then use the Silver remote to change channels.

Bedroom TVs: Simply open the Spectrum App installed on the TV.

Bluetooth Speaker

Find the sliding switch on the side of the speaker and select BA-02 on your phone. Please keep the speaker indoors.

Home Comfort

Air Conditioning

Please keep windows and doors closed while the AC is running. To prevent the system from freezing, set the unit to AUTO/COOL between 70-75 degrees. When checking out, please set it to 65°F.

Refrigerators

It can take up to 48 hours to fully cool after being emptied. Keep the doors closed as much as possible to help it reach temperature.

Vacuum Cleaners

Found in laundry or bedroom closets.

Pools & Hot Tubs

Available after 4:00 p.m. on check-in day. No spray sunscreen near the pool as it damages the filters. No pets in the pool or hot tub. Children must be supervised at all times.

Note: Pool heaters require ambient air above 55°F to operate and cannot be guaranteed during extreme cold.
