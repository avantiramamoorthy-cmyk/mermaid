Troubleshooting

If you experience any issues with the Wi-Fi or connection, a quick restart usually does the trick. To do this, head to the 1st floor TV cabinet and open the second door from the right. Inside, you will find a power extension cord with a RED light switch. Flip this switch to the OFF position (the red light will go out) and wait for at least 30 seconds before flipping it back to ON. Give the system a few minutes to reboot; if you are still having trouble after the restart, please let us know. Our internet provider is Spectrum, and we are happy to help coordinate further support if needed.

How to Use the TVs

We have two large screens located on the Main Floor and the 1st Level. Both are Smart TVs; please remember to sign out of your streaming accounts before you check out!

Main & 1st Level: Power on the cable box using the silver remote (inside center double doors of the TV cabinet). Use the black remote Home button to select inputs: HDMI 1 (Main Floor) or HDMI 2 (1st Level). Use the silver remote for channels.

Bedroom TVs: Simply use the TV remote to navigate to the Spectrum app already installed on the screen.

Bluetooth Speaker

We provide a battery-operated Bluetooth speaker; however, we kindly ask that you do not take the speaker outdoors. Slide the switch to "On" and pair your phone to BA-02.

Pools + Hot Tubs

Pools and hot tubs are serviced on check-in day and are available after 4:00 p.m. Vendor visits mid-week for chemical checks. No pets in pool/hot tub. Children must be supervised.

No spray sunscreen near the pool. It leaves residue, clogs filters, and can damage the pool. Apply away from the water and let it dry first.

Pool Heat (If Applicable)

Operating temperature is 75-85°F. Heaters will not operate if the outside air is below 55°F. Pool heat cannot be guaranteed due to environmental factors or mechanical failure.

Appliances & AC

Refrigerators: May take up to 48 hours to fully cool after being filled. Keep doors shut as much as possible.

Air Conditioners: Controls are on each floor. Please keep windows/doors closed while AC is running. Do not set below 70 degrees on hot days to avoid freezing the system.

Vacuum Cleaners: Located in laundry closets or bedroom closets.
